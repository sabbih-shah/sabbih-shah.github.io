04 / Weather evidenceIndependent R&D

Traceable severe-weather reports

Built a deterministic reporting pipeline for hail and tornado cases. It preserves each finding’s source, time, distance, availability state, and limitations.

In a de-identified Okeene-area run for 6 May 2024, five source families returned matches. The packet contained 124 SWDI radar-hail signatures, four ground reports, six Storm Events records, and observations from four ASOS stations.

  • 0.3 mi nearest report
  • 3.00 in radar estimate
  • 14 / 16 evidence score
  • Address withheld

Interpretation Radar values are probable evidence near the property, not direct property observations. MRMS returned no matching historical file and remained a documented no-match source rather than negative evidence.
